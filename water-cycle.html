Overview

What Is the Water Cycle?

The continuous movement of water around our planet — an infinite loop that never stops.

Step 01
01

Evaporation

The sun heats water in oceans, rivers, and lakes. As it gets hot, water turns into invisible water vapor and rises high into the sky.

Step 02
02

Condensation

High in the cold atmosphere, warm water vapor cools and turns into tiny water droplets. These droplets gather to form big clouds.

Step 03
03

Precipitation

Clouds grow heavy with collected water. When they can hold no more, water falls back to Earth as rain, snow, or hail.

Step 04
04

Collection

Rainwater flows across the land, filling rivers, lakes, and oceans. Soil and plants absorb it — then the cycle begins again.
